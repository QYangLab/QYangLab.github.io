We are the **Language of Thought Lab** at East China Normal University (ECNU), led by *Qianli Yang*.  

Our research explores the computational mechanisms of general intelligence in the brain — bridging **neuroscience**, **artificial intelligence**, and **cognitive science** to understand how minds perceive, reason, and learn.
